# User Interviews

## Status

There are no actual interview transcripts in the workspace yet. This file captures the current hypothesis set and a simple interview plan so the repo still has a structured place for discovery notes.

## Working hypotheses

- Founders want an immediate answer, not a dashboard full of settings.
- Engineering leads care about trust, explainability, and whether the savings numbers are defensible.
- Operators want something they can paste into Slack or email without editing heavily.
- Users are more likely to complete an audit if the first step feels lightweight and low-risk.

## Interview questions

- What triggered the search for an AI spend review?
- Which tools feel hard to justify right now?
- What would make the savings estimate believable?
- What would stop you from sharing the results with your team?
- Would you prefer a quick audit, a deeper consult, or both?
