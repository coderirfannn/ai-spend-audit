# GTM

## Audience

- Founders at small and growing software teams
- Engineering leaders responsible for tooling spend
- Operators who need to trim recurring SaaS and AI costs

## Positioning

AI Spend Audit is positioned as a fast, high-trust way to find hidden AI spend without forcing a full procurement process.

## Core message

- See hidden AI savings instantly.
- Find duplicate tools, idle seats, and pricing leaks.
- Get a shareable report you can review with your team.

## Acquisition channels

- Product-led landing page traffic
- Founder communities and newsletters
- Operator-focused LinkedIn posts
- Demo snippets in engineering and startup communities
- SEO around AI spend, tool consolidation, and seat optimization

## Funnel

1. Visit landing page
2. Start audit
3. Review savings on results page
4. Capture lead for report delivery
5. Share report internally

## Primary conversion goals

- Landing page to audit click-through
- Audit completion rate
- Results page engagement
- Lead capture completion
- Share link creation

## Messaging angles

- Cut AI waste before it compounds
- Replace spreadsheet-based review with a fast audit
- Show a clean, founder-friendly savings story

## Sales motion

Start as a self-serve product. If larger savings are detected, route users toward a deeper review or consultation path.
