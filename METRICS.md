# Metrics

## Primary product metrics

- Landing page visit to audit click-through rate
- Audit form completion rate
- Results page engagement rate
- Lead capture conversion rate
- Share link creation rate

## Quality metrics

- Share page load success rate
- Email delivery success rate
- Results computation success rate
- Persisted draft restore success rate

## Business metrics

- Estimated monthly savings reported per user
- Average savings per completed audit
- High-savings lead rate
- Consultation interest rate

## Technical metrics

- Build success rate
- Test pass rate
- Route error rate
- Database fallback frequency

## Suggested instrumentation

Track events for:

- Landing CTA clicks
- Audit form submission
- Results render completion
- Apply change actions
- Lead capture submission
- Share link open
