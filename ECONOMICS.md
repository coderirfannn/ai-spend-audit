# Economics

## Product economics

The product aims to create value by finding recurring spend that can be reduced immediately or within one purchasing cycle.

## Value model

- Monthly savings are the main user-visible output.
- Annual savings are the executive summary number.
- The report link and lead capture create follow-up and retention value.

## Revenue assumptions

- Self-serve audit can act as a top-of-funnel product.
- High-savings users may convert into consultation or advisory conversations.
- Future monetization could include team plans, advanced benchmarking, or managed reviews.

## Cost structure

- Frontend hosting and build costs
- Database costs for shared snapshots
- Email delivery costs
- Optional model or summarization costs if AI-generated summaries are enabled later

## Unit economics thesis

The product works if the value of one saved tool subscription, one plan downgrade, or one seat reduction materially exceeds the cost of acquiring and serving the user.

## Watchouts

- If audit accuracy is weak, trust and conversion fall quickly.
- If savings numbers are exaggerated, the product becomes harder to defend.
- If report delivery is unreliable, lead capture value collapses.
